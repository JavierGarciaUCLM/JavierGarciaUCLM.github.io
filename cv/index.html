Research

Master's Thesis: Latent Knowledge and Sycophancy in LLMs

In progress · Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC)

Investigates whether language models keep representing the correct answer internally when they yield to user pressure. Combines a philosophical analysis of lying and knowledge with probing of internal truth representations in open-weight models.

Empirical Study of Sycophancy in LLMs

Ongoing · Claude Sonnet, GPT-4o, Llama 3.3 70B

3×3 factorial design (three models, three levels of refutation pressure), 270 annotated interactions, full collection, annotation and statistical analysis pipeline (Cohen's kappa). Preliminary results: capitulation rates up to 26.7% in GPT-4o versus ~4.4% in the other models. IMRaD write-up targeting a scientific venue.

Bachelor's Thesis: Synthetic Empathy in Generative AI: Ethics and Regulation

April 2025 – July 2026 · UCLM / UPV-EHU

Technical analysis of the Transformer architecture (embeddings, self-attention, RLHF) as a mechanism producing responses perceived as empathic without underlying subjective experience. Taxonomy of harm patterns (parasocial attachment, algorithmic paternalism, erosion of decisional autonomy) and six design criteria for emotionally interactive AI.
